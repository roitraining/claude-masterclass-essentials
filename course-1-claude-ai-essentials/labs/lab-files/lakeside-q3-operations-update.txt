Lakeside Outfitters: Q3 Operations Update
Fictional document for teaching only. All names, figures, and terms are invented.

From: Marcus Ellery, Director of Purchasing
To: Operations leadership team
Date: October 2, 2026

Third-quarter operations ran close to plan. Order volume through the e-commerce channel rose 9 percent over the second quarter, driven mostly by the back-to-school and early camping seasons. On-time shipping stayed at 96 percent for the quarter, one point below our 97 percent target, with the three late weeks all traced to a carrier sorting backlog in August.

Warehouse operations stayed stable. The pick-and-pack team handled the higher volume without overtime above budget, and the new carton sizes introduced in July reduced damaged-in-transit claims from 2.1 percent to 1.4 percent. Returns were steady at about 7 percent of orders, and most returned items were resold within two weeks.

Supplier performance was mixed. Ridgeline Packaging delivered every purchase order on time, and its prices held. Two apparel suppliers shipped partial orders in September, which left several winter jackets out of stock during the last week of the quarter. Purchasing is reviewing both suppliers and has asked each for a written recovery plan.

The main decision for this group concerns peak season. Holiday order volume is forecast to run 35 percent above the third quarter. To staff the warehouse for that volume, we need approval by October 20 to hire 12 seasonal workers and add a second evening shift from November 9 through December 24. The estimated cost is $86,000. If approval comes after October 20, training cannot finish before the first heavy week, and we would likely need paid overtime instead, at an estimated $118,000.

Looking ahead, the Ridgeline Packaging agreement renews automatically on January 1, 2028, so no action is needed on it this quarter. Purchasing will report on the apparel supplier recovery plans at the November leadership meeting.
