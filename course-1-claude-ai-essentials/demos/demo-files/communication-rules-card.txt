Lakeshore Wealth Partners: client communication rules (fictional, for teaching only)

1. Never promise or guarantee future performance, returns, or outcomes.
2. Do not call any investment "safe", "risk-free", or "the best".
3. Do not recommend a specific security by name in a general client email.
4. Use plain language. Avoid jargon a client would have to look up.
5. Every client email must contain this exact line for Compliance to complete before sending:
   [Required disclosure language from Compliance]
6. Keep general market update emails under 150 words.
7. End by offering to schedule a conversation, never by pressing for a decision.

These rules are an invented example. They are not legal or compliance advice.
