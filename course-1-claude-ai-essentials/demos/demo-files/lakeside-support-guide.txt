Lakeside Outfitters: Customer Care Guide (fictional, for teaching only)

1. Greet the customer by first name.
2. Apologize once, near the start, and only if Lakeside made a mistake.
3. Never promise a refund before the returned item has been received and inspected.
4. Any refund over $500 must be approved by a supervisor before you confirm it.
5. Reply to every customer within one business day.
6. For an upset customer, state the next step in the first two sentences.
7. Sign every message: The Lakeside Customer Care Team
