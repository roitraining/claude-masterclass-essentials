Lakeside Outfitters: Vendor Contract Playbook (fictional, for teaching only)

Review every vendor supply contract against these six rules.

1. Initial term: no longer than 12 months.
2. Non-renewal notice: no more than 30 days before the end of the term.
3. Price increases: no more than 3 percent, no more than once a year, with at least 90 days' written notice.
4. Early termination fee: no more than 25 percent of the remaining minimum commitment.
5. Payment terms: at least 30 days from the invoice date.
6. Late payment interest: no more than 1.5 percent per month.
