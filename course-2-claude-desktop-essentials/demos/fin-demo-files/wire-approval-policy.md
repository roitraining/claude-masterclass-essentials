# Harborview Supply Co.: Wire Transfer Approval Policy

Fictional policy for teaching only. Version 3, effective September 1, 2026.

## Section 1. Purpose
This policy sets who must approve an outgoing wire transfer before it is released.

## Section 2. Approval levels
2.1 A wire of less than $10,000 needs one approver, who must be an authorized signer.
2.2 A wire of $10,000 up to and including $49,999.99 needs two approvers. At least one must hold the title of Finance Manager or above.
2.3 A wire of $50,000 or more needs two approvers and the written approval of the CFO.
2.4 No one may approve a wire that they requested or prepared.

## Section 3. New beneficiaries
3.1 Before the first wire to a new beneficiary is released, a second employee must confirm the beneficiary's bank details by calling a phone number already on file. An email address or phone number supplied on the payment request does not count.
3.2 The call-back requirement applies at every amount.

## Section 4. International wires
4.1 Every international wire needs approval from a Finance Manager or above, whatever the amount.
4.2 The approver must record that sanctions screening was completed.

## Section 5. Cutoff time
5.1 A wire approved after 3:00 p.m. local time is released on the next business day.

## Section 6. Records
6.1 Finance keeps the approval record for each wire for seven years.
